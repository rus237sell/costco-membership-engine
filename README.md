# Costco membership engine

**Goal:** Test what happens to Costco's operating margin without membership fee income, using its reported 10-K revenue split (net sales vs membership fees) for fiscal 2017 to 2026.

**Macro steps:**
1. Transcribed net sales, membership fees, total revenue, operating income, and net income from Costco's 10-K income statements (FY2017-FY2026); every row checks net sales + membership fees = total revenue.
2. Ran a counterfactual: operating income minus membership fees, expenses held flat. That is an approximate upper bound on the fees' contribution, since membership carries some small cost Costco does not break out; it also does not model losing the members' merchandise spending.
3. Cross-checked total revenue, operating income, and net income live against SEC EDGAR company facts: 10 of 10 fiscal years match within $1M rounding.
4. Wrote the table to `results.csv` and charted reported vs ex-fee operating income (`results.png`).
5. v2: Added replacement sales (extra net sales needed, at that year's ex-fee merchandise margin, to replace all fees) to `results.csv` and `replacement.png`, plus a fee sensitivity table (`sensitivity.csv`: 100/75/50/25/0% realization for every year), and unit tests for the calculations.
6. v3: Added growth attribution (`growth.csv` + `growth.png`): each year's operating income growth split into fee growth vs ex-fee merchandise growth, with cumulative decade totals, plus tests.
7. v4: Added a stability cut (`growth_rates.csv`, `stability.csv` + `stability.png`): YoY growth rates for fees, ex-fee operating income, and reported operating income over FY2018-FY2026, summarized by mean, population standard deviation, range, and down years, plus tests.
8. v5: Added a cumulative / era lens (`cumulative.csv`, `eras.csv` + `cumulative.png`): yearly rows cumulated from FY2017 and pooled into pre-pandemic (FY2017-FY2019), pandemic surge (FY2020-FY2022), and post-surge (FY2023-FY2026) eras, plus tests.
9. v6: Added an incremental-margin cut (`incremental.csv`, `incremental_summary.csv` + `incremental.png`): each year's operating income change per added net sales dollar, split into fee and ex-fee components, plus a decade summary row and tests.
10. v7: Added a margin bridge (`margin_bridge.csv`, `margin_bridge_summary.csv` + `margin_bridge.png`): reported operating margin split into its fee and ex-fee parts, with each part's change vs FY2017 and vs the prior year, plus tests.

**Versions:**
- v1: initial analysis.
- v2: replacement-sales and fee-sensitivity cuts on the same verified table (no new external data), plus tests. Replacement burden fell from 227% of net sales in FY2017 to 102% in FY2026.
- v3: growth-attribution cut on the same table (no new external data). Fees supplied 40.3% of the decade's operating income growth, and over 100% in FY2023 when ex-fee income fell.
- v4: stability cut on the same table (no new external data). Fee growth never fell and stayed in a 5.4% to 11.0% band; ex-fee growth swung -1.0% to +49.5% with about 8x the volatility.
- v5: cumulative / era lens on the same table (no new external data). Fees supplied 57.2% of the decade's operating profit in total; the era fee share fell 70.1% to 58.4% to 52.3% as the ex-fee margin rose in every era.
- v6: incremental-margin / operating-leverage cut on the same table (no new external data). Costco kept 4.43% of each added net sales dollar as operating profit over the decade; fees were just 1.79% of added sales but 40.3% of added profit.
- v7: margin-bridge cut on the same table (no new external data). The reported margin rose 0.67 pts over the decade; the ex-fee part supplied +0.93 pts while the fee part supplied -0.26 pts.

**Short results:** Membership fees are under 2.5% of revenue in every year (1.95% in FY2026), but they equal 50.6% of FY2026 operating income ($5.91B of $11.69B). Without them (expenses held flat), FY2026 operating income halves to $5.78B and the operating margin thins from 3.85% to 1.91%. The dependence is shrinking: fees were 69.4% of operating income in FY2017, and ex-fee operating income grew from $1.26B to $5.78B over the decade, so Costco stayed operating-profitable without fees in all 10 years, thinnest in FY2017-FY2019 at about a 0.9% margin. Fees grew 8.4%/yr against 10.0%/yr for total revenue. Replacement cut: at the ex-fee merchandise margin (1.00% of net sales in FY2017, 1.94% in FY2026), replacing all fees would have taken an extra $286B of net sales in FY2017 (227% of that year's sales, peak 242% in FY2019) and an extra $304B in FY2026 (102%) — the dollars barely moved, the burden halved. At 50% fee realization, FY2026 operating income would still be $8.73B. Growth cut: operating income grew $7.57B from FY2017 to FY2026; fee growth supplied $3.05B (40.3%) and merchandise growth $4.52B, so fees are about half the level of profit but only two fifths of its growth. Fees carried growth early (78% in FY2018, 82% in FY2019) and in FY2023 supplied 111% of growth, because ex-fee income fell $35M while fees rose $356M. Stability cut: over FY2018-FY2026 fee growth averaged 8.4%/yr with a 1.9-pt standard deviation and no down year, while ex-fee operating income grew faster on average (19.5%/yr) but with a 15.6-pt standard deviation, about 8x the fee line's, and one down year — fees are the steady half of profit, merchandise supplies most of the growth and nearly all of the swing. Cumulative cut: over the full decade fees were $41.6B of $72.7B operating income (57.2%, cumulative ex-fee $31.1B); the cumulative fee share peaked at 70.1% through FY2019 and fell every year after, and by era the fee share fell monotonically (70.1% pre-pandemic, 58.4% pandemic surge, 52.3% post-surge) while the ex-fee operating margin rose 0.94% to 1.41% to 1.75% — the falling share is a stronger merchandise business, not a shrinking fee line. Incremental cut: on the $171.1B of net sales added from FY2017 to FY2026, Costco kept $7.57B of operating profit (4.43% incremental margin); fee growth was only $3.05B (1.79% of added sales) but supplied 40.3% of added profit because it drops through with no merchandise cost, and the ex-fee incremental margin (2.64%) also ran above the ex-fee level margin (1.00% in FY2017, 1.94% in FY2026). Annually the total incremental margin swung 2.14% (FY2023) to 9.83% (FY2024), and in FY2023 ex-fee profit fell $35M on $15.0B of added sales, so fees supplied more than all of that year's incremental profit. Margin bridge: the reported operating margin rose from 3.19% in FY2017 to 3.85% in FY2026 (+0.67 pts), and that move splits exactly — the ex-fee margin rose 0.98% to 1.91% (+0.93 pts, 139% of the net change) while the fee margin fell 2.21% to 1.95% (-0.26 pts, trough 1.86% in FY2022), so the margin expanded because the merchandise business improved, not because the fee line did; the reported margin rose in 6 of the 9 growth years, with the largest gain in FY2024 (+0.30 pts, +0.29 of it ex-fee).

Run: `python3 costco_membership.py` (tests: `python3 -m unittest test_costco_membership -v`)
